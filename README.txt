Minigame Name: Bear Lake Battle

Utah Folklore
	There is a monster in Bear Lake, named Isabella. 
	In our minigame, we have a friendlier interpretation of the monster.

Gameplay
	We decided to do a 1v1 duel minigame where each player is riding their own monster. 
	The monsters fight in a sumo battle trying to push each other to the end. 
	The players help their monster push by flexing and punching with correct timing.
	If both players get the same timing judgement, their monsters push with the same force and don't move.
	Whichever player gets a better timing judgement will help their monster push more.
